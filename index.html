Truck types

LED vs static mobile billboards

Both put a large ad on the street. The right choice depends on your message, schedule and budget.

LED / digital

Video, motion and multiple messages

  • Full-motion video or rotating images
  • Bright and readable at night
  • Change creative quickly between days or dayparts
  • Usually costs more per day
Static

One bold message, lower cost

  • Printed vinyl panels on the truck sides
  • Often illuminated for evening visibility
  • Usually lower day rates
  • Printing adds lead time and cost per design

FAQ

Common questions

Are you a mobile billboard company?

No. MobileBillboards.com is a free quote-matching service. We pass your request to independent mobile billboard operators, and they quote and run the campaigns.

Does it cost anything to request quotes?

No. Requesting quotes is free and there's no obligation to book.

How far in advance should I book?

Two to four weeks is comfortable for most campaigns. Allow more time for static trucks (printing) and for major events, holidays or election season, when trucks book up early.

Who sees my information?

We share the details you submit with partner mobile billboard operators so they can send you quotes. See our privacy note.
