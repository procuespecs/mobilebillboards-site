The Philadelphia out-of-home market

Philadelphia ranked #8 among U.S. markets for out-of-home advertising spend in 2025, at about $194 million according to MediaRadar data published by the OAAA. (That figure covers all out-of-home formats, not just mobile billboards.)

Where trucks typically run

Common targets in Philadelphia include Center City and the Pennsylvania Convention Center, Rittenhouse Square, Old City, University City, and the South Philadelphia Sports Complex on game and concert days. The best route depends on your audience, schedule and what local rules allow.

Philadelphia is a big market with a lot of advertising in a compact space. Center City offices, the universities in University City, and the stadiums clustered in South Philadelphia give trucks dense, predictable crowds.

Convention centers and major venues

  • Pennsylvania Convention Center in Center City
  • The South Philadelphia Sports Complex: Lincoln Financial Field, Citizens Bank Park and Xfinity Mobile Arena (formerly the Wells Fargo Center)
  • Franklin Field and the University City campuses

Recurring events to plan around

  • Eagles, Phillies, 76ers and Flyers home games at the South Philadelphia Sports Complex
  • The Mummers Parade on New Year's Day
  • The Penn Relays at Franklin Field each April
  • Conventions and consumer shows at the Pennsylvania Convention Center throughout the year

Local rules to know

Center City streets are narrow and busy, and parking and loading are tightly controlled. Most campaigns there use moving routes rather than parked trucks. The city's sign and outdoor advertising rules are complex, so check local ordinances and confirm with your operator how they handle routes and stops.

This is general information, not legal advice. Rules change, so confirm current requirements with your operator and the local authority before you book.

Typical costs

Typical day rates reported by industry pricing guides are roughly $500–$2,400 for static trucks and $800–$5,000+ for LED trucks, for a standard 8-hour day. Large, high-demand markets often fall toward the upper end, especially on major event dates. These are general ranges, not quotes. See mobile billboard cost for what affects price.
