The Atlanta out-of-home market

Atlanta ranked #9 among U.S. markets for out-of-home advertising spend in 2025, at about $165 million according to MediaRadar data published by the OAAA. (That figure covers all out-of-home formats, not just mobile billboards.)

Where trucks typically run

Common targets in Atlanta include Midtown, Buckhead, Downtown, the Georgia World Congress Center, and stadium events. The best route depends on your audience, schedule and what local rules allow.

Atlanta hosts major conventions and sporting events throughout the year, which makes event-focused truck bookings common.

Typical costs

Typical day rates reported by industry pricing guides are roughly $500–$2,400 for static trucks and $800–$5,000+ for LED trucks, for a standard 8-hour day. Large, high-demand markets often fall toward the upper end, especially on major event dates. These are general ranges, not quotes. See mobile billboard cost for what affects price.
