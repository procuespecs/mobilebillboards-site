LED vs static mobile billboards

Screens or printed panels? A side-by-side look at the two main mobile billboard formats.

Get free quotes

Most mobile billboard operators offer two main formats: LED (digital) trucks with screens, and static trucks with printed panels. Here's how they compare.

LED / digitalStatic
DisplayLED screens: video, animation, rotating imagesPrinted vinyl panels, often illuminated
Night visibilityExcellentGood when lit
Changing creativeFast: upload new filesRequires printing and installing new panels
Multiple advertisersSome operators offer shared or rotating slotsUsually one advertiser per truck
Typical day rate~$800 – $5,000+~$500 – $2,400
Best forEvents, night campaigns, multiple messages, videoOne strong message, longer runs, lower budgets

Price ranges are typical figures reported by industry pricing guides (2026), not quotes.

When LED makes sense

  • Your campaign runs after dark or at night events
  • You want video, motion or several messages
  • You need to change creative quickly, for example day-by-day promotions

When static makes sense

  • You have one clear, bold message
  • You're running for weeks or months and want a lower day rate
  • Your creative won't change during the campaign
