Privacy

Privacy note

Short and plain.

What we collect

When you request quotes, we collect what you enter in the form: name, company, email, phone, campaign cities, dates, budget range, truck type and message, plus the page you submitted from.

How we use it

We share your request with partner mobile billboard operators so they can contact you with availability and quotes. We may also contact you about your request. We don't sell your information for unrelated marketing.

Form processing

Form submissions are delivered by email using a third-party form service (FormSubmit). See their policies at formsubmit.co.

Questions or removal

To ask about or remove your information, email quotes@mobilebillboards.com.
